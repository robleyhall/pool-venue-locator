# Source provenance

The dataset records publicly documented pool venues, league hosts and event
locations. Each record links to its supporting source and labels uncertainty.
Venue addresses are business addresses. Review dates describe when evidence
was examined, not a guarantee of current operating status.

The initial release contains 60 venues across eight markets and nine KML files.
Austin evidence was reviewed October 24, 2026; other markets retain their
August 18, 2026 review dates. Coordinate provenance and precision limitations
are documented in [data conventions](DATA.md) and individual records.

On October 26, 2026, two omissions were added to Southern New Hampshire /
Regional Reach, bringing the directory to 62 venues. Sources read:

- [Legends' official billiards page](https://legendsportsmouth.com/billiards/):
  address and twelve nine-foot tables.
- [Forest City's official site](https://forestcitybilliards.com/): 23 Gabriels
  Sentinel pool tables, twelve-foot snooker table and regional tournament role.
- [JPNEWT 2026 event dates](https://www.jpnewt.com/2026-dates): Forest City's
  address and nine-foot play for the advertised November 7–8 event.

Only these two venue records were reviewed in this update. Other venues keep
their earlier evidence dates. Market refresh dates reflect the latest record
update, as defined in [data conventions](DATA.md#market-refresh-dates).
